Procedural mode: the generator now produces new landscapes, not a copy of one window

The requirement is a mod whose terrain generation produces reference-like scenery in some regions,
so a fixed list of massif coordinates was the wrong model. FitParams gains a procedural mode:

  * one candidate landform per cell (128 blocks), sampled from the distributions measured on the
    fitted skeleton: peaks 75%, basins 30%, radius 73..143, elongation 0.72..1.90, amplitude 73..165,
    orientation uniform
  * contributions summed over the 3x3 cell neighbourhood, so landforms blend
  * a slow region mask (period ~2000 blocks) decides where the style applies at all, i.e. "certain
    regions" rather than everywhere
  * heights clamped to the world before filling

Verified at a fresh location (4096,4096), which has nothing to do with the reference's coordinates:

    metric        reference   procedural
    relief            238        245
    snow %           63.9       57.5
    ridges/km2      278.5      141.6
    anisotropy       85.6      139.6
    slope p50/p90   0.71/1.5   0.50/1.0
    slope p99        2.50      10.51   (too many small cliffs)
    water %          0.59       3.30

That is the first honest generalisation measurement in this project: a landscape the fit never saw,
produced from distributions rather than positions.
