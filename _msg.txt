Ridge segments + per-chunk slope limiter in the mod: distance 0.251 on an unseen location

Ported the ridge primitive and the angle-of-repose limiter into the generator:

- landforms are now segments (ridge lines) with a power-law profile, sampled per cell like before
- the limiter needs neighbours, so the chunk generator evaluates the analytic field on a 16+10 padded
  patch, relaxes it (max 2.5 blocks/block, 4 sweeps) and fills the inner 16x16. The patch is fixed
  relative to the chunk, so neighbouring chunks agree in their overlap and no seams appear.
- the detail layer is multi-octave (3 octaves) and quieter on steep ground

Measured at (4096,4096), a location the fit never saw:

    metric        reference   generated
    relief            238        238
    slope p50        0.71       0.71
    slope p90        1.50       1.50
    slope p99        2.50       2.55
    flat %          13.54      14.04
    water %          0.59       0.61
    snow %          63.88      57.71
    ridges/km2      278.5      256.3
    anisotropy       85.6       99.2

    distance 0.251   (previous best 0.489, datapack 0.87)

That is the first time the generated terrain matches the reference's shape statistics at a location
it was not fitted to.
