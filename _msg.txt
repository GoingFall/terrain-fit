2x training run: 88.9k steps in 40 minutes, but samples still collapse toward the mean

Retrained at 2x downsampling (64x64 crops covering 128 blocks, 9928 crops) after the 4x data turned out
to be featureless. On the GPU: 88,860 steps in 2400 s, loss 0.0060 (against 0.0101 at 4x, which is the
expected direction: more structure in the data).

Comparing samples with real crops at the same scale (preview/images/layout_2x_vs_real.png):

    sampled tile std   0.06 - 0.13
    real crop std      0.09 - 0.13

and visually the samples have the right kind of mottling but none of the slope faces: the real crops
show large coherent gradients, the samples are flatter and rounder.

Diagnosis: the per-crop variance is small compared with the unit-variance noise the model is trained to
predict. In [-1, 1] space a crop has std about 0.1, so the optimal epsilon prediction is dominated by
zero -- predicting "noise" is easy and collapses toward the mean. Two standard remedies:
  * standardise each crop (subtract its own mean, divide by its own std) and supply the global level and
    relief from a separate low-frequency field, so the model only has to learn shape
  * predict v instead of epsilon, which behaves better when the data range is narrow

Both are small changes to the training script, and at 40 minutes per run they are cheap to test.
