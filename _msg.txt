Timing a layout diffusion model: 8 minutes is not enough, and the numbers say why

tools/train_layout_diffusion.py trains a 0.24M parameter DDPM on 64x64 crops of the reference map
downsampled 4x (400 crops). Measured on this machine, CPU only:

    steps/second          4.8      (32 threads, batch 16)
    8 minute run          2293 steps, loss 0.0128
    sampling              0.67 s per 64x64 tile -> 2.9 min for a 512x512 field

The samples after 8 minutes are grey blobs: the model has learned the marginal height distribution and
nothing else. Too few steps for this architecture, not a bug.

Extrapolating the cost of a usable model:

    budget            steps    expected state
    8 min             2.3k     marginal distribution only
    1 hour            17k      coarse structure appearing
    4 hours           69k      plausible layouts
    1 day             400k     diminishing returns

So training a layout model on CPU is an overnight job, not an afternoon one. The GPU is idle because
the installed torch is the CPU build; an RTX 5070 would need torch built for CUDA 12.8 (Blackwell),
about a 2.5 GB download, and would plausibly cut the step time by one to two orders of magnitude.
