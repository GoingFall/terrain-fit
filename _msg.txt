Surface pass: rock on steep ground, snow as a layer, and a mid-scale roughness term

Three changes to what the player actually sees:

1. surface by local slope: gradient > rockSlope (1.6) keeps bare stone, otherwise grass; snow only
   settles where the ground is gentler than the same threshold. That is what stops high ground from
   reading as one white blanket.
2. snow is drawn as a snow *layer* above the ground instead of replacing the surface block, so grass
   shows through on gentle ground the way it does on the reference map.
3. a mid-scale noise term (midAmp 10, wavelength ~150 blocks) fills the 64-128 Hz gap that the
   spectrum comparison had flagged as empty; secondary ridges now exist between the main ones.

Also fixed: the slope limiter was applied on a chunk-relative patch, and because the relaxation is
order dependent, neighbouring chunks disagreed across their border -- the seams showed up as straight
lines in the top-down view. Replaced with a closed-form 5-point smoother that depends only on
absolute position, so any two chunks agree by construction (measured border bias down from visible
steps to +0.15 blocks).

Measured on r.8.8 after the change: relief 222, slope p50/p90/p99 0.71/1.41/2.06, snow 54%, water
2.2%, ridges 217/km2 (reference: 238, 0.71/1.50/2.50, 64%, 0.6%, 278).
