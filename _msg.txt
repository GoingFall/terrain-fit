Disable structures; the straight lines were cone creases, not chunk seams

1. Villages were generating inside the custom dimension (dirt_path 1533 columns, 197x223 blocks) and
   showed up in the preview as crosses and sand patches. structure_overrides is part of the vanilla
   noise generator's codec and does nothing for our own ChunkGenerator, so the fix is to override
   createStructures/createReferences to no-ops. dirt_path is now zero and unknown blocks dropped from
   11k to 177 (the 11k came from a mushroom_fields experiment that spawned huge mushrooms).

2. The straight lines in the top-down are not chunk seams. Measuring |dx| per column: the biggest
   jumps are at x=383 (6.5 blocks), x=181 and x=63 -- only two of those are chunk borders, and the
   chunk-border bias is +0.15 blocks. They are the creases where two truncated cones meet: the
   landforms are cones, and their intersection traces a straight line.

So the remaining visual problem is the primitive's shape, not a bug: large planar facets with
straight edges, whereas the reference has curved ridge lines and slopes that change character along
the ridge. Next step would be curved ridge polylines (bend each segment) plus more mid-scale noise to
break up the planar facets.
